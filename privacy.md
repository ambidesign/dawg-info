## 4. Artificial Intelligence Features and Data Sharing

Certain features of the Services use artificial intelligence provided by third-party AI service providers. We currently use **OpenAI (United States)** to power features including the AI coaching chat and AI-based food-photo nutritional estimation.

**What we send to AI providers.** When you use these features, we may transmit: (a) your user-generated inputs (such as chat messages and prompts, and photographs you submit for food/nutrition analysis); (b) limited contextual data necessary to generate a relevant response (such as your current habits or program names, or dietary context); and (c) technical metadata necessary to operate and secure the feature. We follow data-minimization principles and send only what is reasonably necessary to fulfill your request.

**What we do not intentionally send.** We do not intentionally transmit your full legal name, address, government identifiers, payment information, or contacts to AI providers. **You should not submit sensitive personal information, confidential information, or information about other people through AI features.** When you submit a photograph, you are responsible for ensuring it does not contain another person's personal information without their consent.

**How AI providers use the data.** AI providers process the data on our behalf to generate responses and estimates and to maintain the reliability, safety, and performance of their systems. We do not permit our AI providers to sell your personal information, to use it for their own advertising, or to build profiles of you unrelated to providing the feature. We access these providers through their business or API services. Under OpenAI's API terms, inputs and outputs submitted through the API are not used to train OpenAI's general-purpose models, and OpenAI retains API data only for a limited period for purposes such as abuse monitoring, security, debugging, and legal compliance, as described in its applicable terms and policies. AI inputs may be processed in the United States and in other jurisdictions where the provider operates. Third-party providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

**We do not train our own models on your private content.** We do not use your private AI chats, food photographs, journal or mood entries, weight history, or nutrition logs to train or fine-tune our own AI models, unless we first obtain your consent or de-identify the data so that it cannot reasonably identify you.

**Access to AI data.** Our personnel and service providers may access AI inputs and outputs only to the extent reasonably necessary to operate, debug, secure, and support the Services and to investigate safety or abuse concerns.

**Retention.** We retain AI chat history that is cached on your device until you clear it or delete the App. Food photographs submitted for AI nutrition analysis are sent to our AI service provider, currently OpenAI, for the purpose of generating nutrition estimates. We do not store food photographs on our servers after the analysis is complete and we do not maintain a persistent food-photo library. The resulting nutrition estimate, meal entry, calories, macros, and related information may be saved to your meal logs if you choose to add them.

**Your controls.** You can stop using AI features at any time, clear your AI chat history within the App, decline to submit photographs, and delete associated content (subject to technical limitations). See Section 9 for your broader rights.

**Important.** AI output may be inaccurate and is not professional medical, nutritional, mental-health, legal, or financial advice. See our Terms of Use, Sections 13 and 14.
